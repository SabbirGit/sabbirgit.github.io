Research notes

Why do employees keep using a dashboard?

Illustrative theory draft

Scope — Employees who can choose whether to use an organizational analytics dashboard.

Proposition — Employees who perceive dashboard data as more reliable are more likely to keep using it.

Proposed mechanism — Employees who trust the data expect to spend less time verifying it before using it in their work.
